Gift Ideas

No pressure — pick anything that feels useful from the list below.

Diapering & Organization

  • Diapers in larger sizes (Size 1, 2, or 3 instead of only Newborn, which babies outgrow quickly)
  • Fragrance-free baby wipes (bulk packs)
  • Portable changing pads (foldable and wipeable for on-the-go diaper changes)
  • Diaper caddies (for organizing wipes, creams, and diapers around the house)
  • Diaper bags (backpacks or totes with insulated bottle pockets)
  • Diaper rash cream

Feeding & Burping

  • Muslin burp cloths (absorbent and soft for frequent spit-ups)
  • Silicone bibs (with food-catching pockets for starting solids)
  • Bottle warmers
  • Bottle sterilizers (electric steam or UV sterilizers)
  • Bottle drying racks and brush sets
  • Silicone food feeders (for introducing fresh fruits and soft foods safely)
  • Baby spoons and suction bowls

Sleep & Comfort

  • Wearable blankets / sleep sacks (safer than loose blankets in the crib)
  • Breathable muslin swaddles (multi-use for swaddling, nursing covers, or stroller shade)
  • White noise machines
  • Dim night lights (for middle-of-the-night feedings and diaper changes)
  • Fitted crib sheets (extra sets for quick middle-of-the-night changes)

Bath & Clothing

  • Hooded bath towels
  • Soft baby washcloths
  • Gentle, tear-free baby wash and lotion
  • Two-way zip sleepers / footie pajamas (for easier midnight diaper changes without fully undressing the baby)
  • Baby socks and mittens
